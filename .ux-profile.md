# UX profile — consciouscafe.in

Read by the VectorLab UX skills (ux-audit and the rest) before they score a screen.

- **Aesthetic:** calm, editorial and specific to the place. Limewash page (`#f5f5f0`), green-black ink, forest-green brand, real photographs. One bold moment: the red-oxide kolam band on the home page. No glass, gradients, or decoration for its own sake.
- **Type:** EB Garamond for headlines and reading text; Anek Tamil (Ek Type) for everything you operate. Labels and buttons are sentence case with no letter-spacing. The minimum is 12px, with 14px or more for operating text. Spacing is on a 4px grid. Prose lines are at most 68ch.
- **Voice:** plain and kind, in Indian English. Buttons start with a verb. Never blame the visitor. Headlines carry no single italic accent word.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA. Muted text `#5e665a` is 5.4:1 on the page. Hit targets are at least 44×44px.
- **Motion:** only transform and opacity animate, for 100–300ms, on what a person does. Reduced motion turns it off.

## Intentional deviations (do not flag)

- **The wordmark** "CONSCIOUS CAFÉ" (nav and phone menu) stays in tracked capitals. It's the logo, not a label.
- **The kolam draws once over about 2.8s** when it scrolls into view (`components/common/Kolam/`). It's the page's single orchestrated moment and is still under reduced motion.

(2026-11-26: this replaces the earlier "letter-spaced capitals are house style" entry. Both the design pass and the UX checklist chose sentence case.)
