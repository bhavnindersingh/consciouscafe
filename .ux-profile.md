# UX profile — consciouscafe.in

Read by the VectorLab UX skills (ux-audit and the rest) before they score a screen.

- **Aesthetic:** calm, editorial and warm. Paper backgrounds, ink text, one forest-green accent, serif display type, real photos. No glass, gradients or decoration for its own sake.
- **Voice:** plain and kind, in Indian English. Buttons start with a verb. Never blame the visitor.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA. Hit targets are at least 44×44px.
- **Type:** 12px minimum. Spacing is on a 4px grid. Prose lines are at most 68ch.
- **Motion:** only transform and opacity animate, for 100–300ms. Reduced motion turns it off.

## Intentional deviations (do not flag)

- **Capitals on buttons and small labels.** Buttons, tabs, eyebrows and field labels show as letter-spaced capitals ("SEND FOR REVIEW"). This is the house style across the whole site (home, menu, story, sanctuary). The capitals come only from CSS (`text-transform: uppercase`). The words in the code are written in sentence case ("Send for review"), so screen readers and copied text read them normally. Keep these labels short (about four words or fewer). Never put a sentence, an error, or anything a person typed (a name, a place, a title) in capitals.
